## Category 1: PURPOSE & SCOPE

### Q1.1: What is the purpose of the health and safety policy?
**Expected Answer**: The purpose is to outline Nexora Inc.'s commitment to providing a safe, secure, and healthy work environment for all employees, contractors, visitors, and stakeholders. The company is committed to preventing workplace injuries, illnesses, and incidents by proactively identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks associated with business activities.

### Q1.2: Who does this policy apply to?
**Expected Answer**: The policy applies to all individuals working for or on behalf of Nexora Inc., including full-time, part-time, and temporary employees, independent contractors, vendors, and visitors. It covers all facilities, remote work locations, company vehicles, and off-site locations where company business is conducted.

### Q1.3: Is this policy relevant for remote workers?
**Expected Answer**: Yes, the policy explicitly states it covers remote work locations in addition to company facilities.

## Category 2: CORE COMMITMENTS

### Q2.1: What are Nexora's core commitments to health and safety?
**Expected Answer**: Nexora commits to: (1) Compliance with all applicable health and safety laws, regulations, and industry standards; (2) Risk Management through robust systems to identify hazards and implement control measures; (3) Continuous Improvement through regular audits and inspections; (4) Training and Awareness for all personnel; (5) Consultation and Participation with employees and their representatives.

### Q2.2: How does Nexora handle risk management?
**Expected Answer**: Nexora implements robust systems to identify hazards, assess risks, and implement effective control measures to eliminate or minimize risks to the lowest reasonably practicable level.

## Category 3: RESPONSIBILITIES

### Q3.1: What are the responsibilities of executive management?
**Expected Answer**: Executive Management is ultimately responsible for the health and safety of all personnel. They must allocate adequate resources, establish clear objectives, and demonstrate visible leadership in promoting a strong safety culture throughout the organization.

### Q3.2: What are managers and supervisors required to do?
**Expected Answer**: Managers and supervisors are responsible for ensuring work environments under their control are safe and compliant. They must ensure hazards are identified and controlled, employees receive appropriate training, and safety rules are enforced consistently.

### Q3.3: What are employees and contractors responsible for?
**Expected Answer**: All employees and contractors have a personal responsibility to work safely, follow established safety procedures, use provided protective equipment, and report any hazards, incidents, or near misses immediately to their supervisor or the safety department. No individual should ever perform a task they believe is unsafe.

---

## Category 4: HAZARD IDENTIFICATION & RISK ASSESSMENT

### Q4.1: When is hazard identification required?
**Expected Answer**: Proactive hazard identification and risk assessment is required prior to the commencement of any new project, introduction of new equipment or processes, or significant changes to existing operations.

### Q4.2: What happens after hazards are identified?
**Expected Answer**: All identified hazards must be documented, and appropriate control measures must be implemented according to the hierarchy of controls: elimination, substitution, engineering controls, administrative controls, and personal protective equipment.

---

## Category 5: INCIDENT REPORTING

### Q5.1: What must be reported?
**Expected Answer**: All workplace incidents, accidents, injuries, illnesses, and near misses, regardless of severity, must be reported immediately.

### Q5.2: Who investigates incidents?
**Expected Answer**: The Health and Safety Department will lead investigations into all reported events to determine root causes and implement corrective actions to prevent recurrence.

### Q5.3: Is there protection for those who report safety concerns?
**Expected Answer**: Yes, retaliation against any individual who reports a safety concern in good faith is strictly prohibited.
